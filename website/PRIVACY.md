# Your scenes. Your device.

This policy describes how GAPCO Limited Liability Company handles information in Liminal Generator and on these support pages.

## Using the app

The app generates music and processes bundled images on your device. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives your music, videos, settings, or app activity. The app includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

## Videos and sharing

Rendered videos are created in the app’s temporary storage. When you choose to save or share a video, iOS passes it to the destination you select. That destination’s privacy policy applies. We do not receive a copy. Removing the app deletes its local data; copies you saved or shared must be deleted from their respective destinations. iOS may also clear temporary files.

## Optional tips

Apple processes tips through in-app purchases under its own privacy policy. The app receives product and transaction information from StoreKit to verify and finish a purchase on your device. We do not receive your payment card details, and the app does not send transaction information to a developer-operated server. Apple may provide us with sales reports and diagnostics through its developer services, subject to Apple’s policies and your settings.

## Support correspondence

If you email hello@thegapco.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Our email service provider processes correspondence to deliver and store it. We retain it only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us; we may retain information where legally required. Do not include sensitive payment details.

## This website and external services

These pages are hosted by GitHub. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to operate and secure its service. We add no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies to these pages. External links and Apple’s services are governed by their own policies: [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your choices and contact

You can use all creative features without tipping or contacting us. You choose whether and where to share a video. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you supplied to us, contact [hello@thegapco.com](mailto:hello@thegapco.com).

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
