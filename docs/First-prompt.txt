I want to design an iOS app called 'Liminal Generator' - a super simple, free app that allows users to generate liminal music and visuals. The main screen would look like a simple card that says 'Liminal Generator' in the top left, with a randomly selected image of an empty space, such as an airport terminal, shopping mall, office filled with empty cubicles, etc. This image can be swiped l/r to index between a fairly large library of similar images - all the images should have a filter to make them look like they are on VHS video - Beneath this image the settings would involve regenerating random simple arpeggio lines in the liminal style, with sliders below to determine space (reverb) level, age (tape wow/flutter effect), and a toggle to turn on/off drums - when turned on there would be a slider for level that would determine its volume, with another generate (dice?) button to regenerate different lo-fi beats. The Space and age sliders would be global, so they would affect both the synth and drum layers. There should be a mechanism to render and share 2 minute clips with fade in/out. For context on liminal music, please refer to this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-TevMSwEuSs
