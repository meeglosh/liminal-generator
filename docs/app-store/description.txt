Make music for places that feel like a memory.

Liminal Generator pairs generative ambient music with VHS-treated images of quiet, empty spaces. Start a new melody, add a little bass or a lo-fi beat, and shape the sound until the scene feels right.

CREATE YOUR OWN ATMOSPHERE
• Generate warm chord progressions and sparse melodies on your iPhone.
• Add optional basslines and choose from 10 lo-fi drum loops.
• Shape tone, space, tape age, chorus, and speed with tactile controls.
• Swipe through 23 bundled liminal scenes with animated VHS effects.

MAKE A TWO-MINUTE VIDEO
Render your selected scene and music into a square video with VHS texture, a retro timestamp, and a Liminal Generator watermark. When drums are enabled, occasional drum breaks let the keys and bass breathe. Save or share using the iOS share sheet.
