Make a scene

Tap Play to listen. Generate Melody creates a new chord progression and melody. Swipe the image to change the setting. Add optional bass and lo-fi drums, then shape the tone, space, age, nostalgia, and speed.

Render and share

Render & Share creates a two-minute video of the selected scene and music. Keep the app open while it renders. Use the iOS share sheet to save or send your clip. After closing the sheet, Re-share opens the same video again.

Tips are always optional

The tip jar is in About and on the completed-render screen. Tips are one-time App Store purchases, not subscriptions, and do not unlock features. Prices are displayed in your local currency. If an amount is unavailable, check your connection and try again.

Apple handles payments and refund requests. To request a refund, visit Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Your privacy

Music is generated on your device. No account, advertising, or third-party analytics SDK is required. Read our privacy policy for details about purchases, sharing, and contacting support.
